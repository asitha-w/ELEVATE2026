# Team Composition — Draft 1 · 16 Jul 2026

Source of truth for the site's **Teams** tab. Person-level roster (with reporting
lines) is kept internally — this public draft carries team names and pool sizes only.

## The rules

1. **AI teams are set aside** — they already build AI; they staff the expert crews
   and mentor bench instead of competing.
2. A project with **4 or more members** stands alone as a team.
3. Projects with **fewer than 4** combine into **domain teams**.
4. **Every team fields exactly 3 builders** on event night — the rest of the project
   joins at ~8 PM for the pitches, awards and dinner.
5. Same-customer micro-rows join their own project's pool (BUS Research → BUS,
   Solwr OMS-Retail → Solwr OMS-Main).
