1
5:00 PM · together

Brief & calibrate

Read the anchors board. Agree what a 0, 3 and 5 look like on each step of the card. Split into 2 crews — one per floor.

Same rubric all evening — teams have had it since registration.
2
5–7:30 PM · your floor

Mentor laps

Visit tables, unblock, challenge. No scores yet — teams are meant to iterate on what you say. Watch who iterates.

Progression is signal: a team that moved hard since kickoff is showing the behavior we reward.
3
~7:30 PM · scoring lap

Score the floor card

Same visit everywhere: ~10 min, five checks in order — the pain · the mapping · the agent · the gate · the proof. Score before the next table, no table-talk.

First: is the pain point real? Is it mapped to the right surface? Only then the details.
4
~8:00 PM · floor closes

Rank & advance

Rank within your floor — never across. Top 4 per floor advance: 8 finalists. Hand each 2–3 written notes worth fixing before the pitch.

Floor scores are banked for the five surface awards — every team stays in play.
5
~8:15 PM · cafe stage

Pitch & pick

All judges see all 8. Fresh scores on the pitch card. Ties break on Trustworthy Autonomy. Pick ~3 winners — and list every entry ready to offer a customer.

Winners go to the 99x expert panel, then to the customer as a real offering.
