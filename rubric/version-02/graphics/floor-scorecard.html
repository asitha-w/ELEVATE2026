1
The Pain
Is this a pain point worth elevating?
Invented for the demo, or trivial — nobody would pay to remove it
Real but peripheral; the team can name who feels it
Real, recurring, costly — the project's actual bottleneck; team can say what it costs today
2
The Mapping
Pain → declared pillar → measure: does the chain hold?
Pain and declared pillar don't match; no measure named
Right pillar, fuzzy measure
Clean chain with a defined before/after measure — a real elevation path
3
The Agent
What role does the human play?
Human is the operator — prompt chain, human drives every step
Agent runs multi-step work but needs babysitting
Human is the approver; 5 = autonomy deliberately matched to the stage's risk
4
The Gate
Where does the human decide — show it firing
No gate, or a confirm-button the team can't explain
Gate at a sensible point; human sees what the agent proposes
Shows why (evidence, diff) · reject/edit incorporated · at the consequential action
5
The Proof
Did the declared pillar actually move?
Slideware, mock data, or movement merely asserted
Rough run on the real project; movement plausible, warts visible — projected with visible math lands here
Measured movement on the declared pillar; repeated runs, candid on failures & cost
6
The Offering
Extended Value: is this real beyond the demo?
No named user, no workflow, no cost story
Named user & workflow; rough cost-to-run; honest gap list
Named user + workflow + unit economics + transferable pattern — 99x could scope the offering from this visit
