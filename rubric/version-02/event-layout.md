## The floor (`graphics/building-plan.png` — to be redrawn)

The new venue's larger floor hosts the **entire event in one sitting**: all
29 team pods, the judges' walkway, dinner service, and the sharing stage —
no floor changes, no room changes, all evening.

| Zone | Role |
|---|---|
| Team pods | 29 tables in five route blocks (A–E, ~6 tables each) — one judge pair per route |
| Stage end | Kickoff 5:00 · Judges' Pick announcement 8:15 · knowledge sharing 8:20 |
| Dinner line | Opens ~7:45, same floor — teams eat at/near their pods |
| Judges' corner | Pair huddles between laps · full-panel deliberation ~7:45–8:15 |

## Evening timeline

| Time | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 5:00 | Kickoff | Everyone, stage end |
| 5:15 | Build — mentor laps all evening, no scores | Teams + 5 judge pairs roving |
| 7:00–8:00 | **Scoring lap** — ~10 min/station, six steps in order | Each pair scores its ~6-station route |
| ~7:45 | Dinner opens · panel deliberation begins (10 shortlisted → 8) | Dinner line · judges' corner |
| 8:00 | Rest of the company joins | Whole floor |
| 8:15 | **Judges' Pick 8 announced** — every team handed its 2–3 written notes | Stage end |
| 8:20–8:55 | **Knowledge sharing** — the eight teach the floor, ~4–5 min each: the build story · the number · the gate firing · what you can copy Monday | Stage end, whole company |
| ~8:55 | Close — expert-panel forward list · the North Star, word for word | Stage end |

Pacing check: 5 pairs × ~6 stations × ~10 min = the scoring lap fits exactly in
7:00–8:00; the old two-crew feasibility concern (13–14 tables per crew) is gone.

## Team pod (`graphics/team-area.png` — unchanged layout)

One table = one team's stage. 3 builders per pod (team plan v3); **one laptop
serves as the demo screen**; **name plate facing the judges' walkway**; power
strip; sodas & water within reach.

Pod checklist (printed on the graphic): plate visible ☐ · demo laptop ready ☐ ·
gate on screen ☐ · before/after number written down ☐ · power ☐.

The 10-minute visit, in order (what teams should be ready for): 1) What's the
pain? 2) Which pillar, which measure? 3) Show the agent working. 4) Show the
gate firing. 5) Show the pillar moved. 6) Who runs this on Monday, and what's
one run worth?

## Name plate (`graphics/team-nameplate.png` — to be reprinted)

**The plate is the judges' team ID** — printed A4 landscape, one per team, faces
the walkway all evening. Fields (write-in):

- **Team** (big — readable from the walkway)
- **Project & customer** (the real project this elevates)
- **Lifecycle stage the agent owns** (discovery / construction / operation / adoption)
- **Pillars declared — circle 1 or 2** (four chips, North Star V1.1 names:
  Higher Pace · Better Quality · Extended Value · A Team That Thrives)
- **Driver metric + baseline** (per the Team Guide doctrine)
- **The pain we're elevating — one line**
- **Route · Table** (floor field dropped — there is only one)

Tagline on the band: **"Agents deliver. You decide."** — official, on the launch
flyer.
