Score the elevation, not the agent.

The judging & event pack — how the evening runs, how teams are scored, and what to have ready at your table. An agentic solution is measured by its effectiveness on the project, never by the complexity of the agent.

1One building, one evening

Teams build on floors 1 & 5, each floor with its own roving expert crew. Dinner on floor 6 & cafe. Everything converges on the cafe stage: 8 finalists pitch, ~3 winners — and the best entries go to the 99x expert panel, then to the customer as a real offering.

2For judges — the guide and the anchors

Five steps from the floor to the podium, and the anchors board: the five surfaces PaceQualityValuePartnershipHappiness — what the pain looks like, the elevation path, and the proof that counts.

3The two scorecards

Level 1 (floor): five steps in order — the pain · the mapping · the agent · the gate · the proof (0–5 each, /25). Rank within your floor, never across; top 4 per floor advance. Level 2 (pitch): the number · the offering · the stress test · beyond the demo (/20), fresh scores.

Qualifying bar to advance: genuinely agentic (a prompt chain is not an agent) · ran on the real project (rough is fine, mock is not) · surface declared (scored there only).

4For teams — your table is your stage

Judges walk the floor, read the plate, and say "run it now." Set the pod up so that's a 30-second ask — and keep the name plate facing the walkway: it's how judges ID your team.
